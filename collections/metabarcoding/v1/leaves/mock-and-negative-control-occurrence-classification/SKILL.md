# mock-and-negative-control-occurrence-classification

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Classifies ASV/variant occurrences in metabarcoding samples as true or false positives using known-composition mock community samples and negative controls, then computes sensitivity TP/(TP+FN) and precision TP/(TP+FP) to benchmark filtering pipelines such as VTAM, DALU, and OBIbaR.

## When to use

Use when metabarcoding ASV/variant tables from replicated samples include mock community and negative control samples, and you need to score filtering pipelines by sensitivity and precision against expected (ground-truth) occurrences.

## When NOT to use

- The dataset lacks mock community samples or negative controls, since TP/FP classification cannot be established without expected-composition and blank samples
- You only have an already-filtered ASV table without replicate or control-sample metadata, so occurrence reproducibility and false-positive status cannot be assessed
- The goal is raw denoising/clustering quality of a single-sample study rather than cross-pipeline benchmarking of filtering against controls

## Inputs

- Trimmed, demultiplexed FASTQ/FASTA files from the bat (Galan et al. 2018), fish (Corse et al. 2017), and shark (Esposito et al. 2022) datasets
- Mock community samples and negative control samples with known expected/absent taxa
- COInr COI and RDP Classifier 16S trainsetNo18 reference databases for taxassign
- Per-dataset optimized VTAM filter parameter sets (lfn_read_count_cutoff, pcr_error_var_prop, lfn_sample_replicate_cutoff, lfn_variant_cutoff)

## Outputs

- out/summary_min_readcount_XX/control_counts.tsv with per-occurrence true/false positive classifications
- Sensitivity (TP/(TP+FN)) and precision (TP/(TP+FP)) values per pipeline (VTAM, DALU, OBIbaR), dataset, marker (COI/16S), and read-count cut-off (0/10/40/60)
- Sensitivity–precision plots generated by scripts/plt_sensitivity_precision.py

## How to apply

Run each pipeline on the same trimmed, demultiplexed fastq/fasta inputs: VTAM filter with per-dataset optimized parameters (bat: lfn_read_count_cutoff 60, pcr_error_var_prop 0.2, lfn_sample_replicate_cutoff 0.002, lfn_variant_cutoff 0.006; fish: 10, 0.1, 0.003, 0.006; shark: 40, 0.2, 0.001, 0.016 with FilterCodonStop/FilterIndel skipped) plus taxassign; DALU (DADA2 denoising, LULU curation, acceptance in ≥2 replicates) and OBIbaR (OBITools3 + metabaR). Sweep the minimum read count cut-off over 0, 10, 40, and 60, mirroring VTAM's suggested cut-offs (10 fish, 40 shark, 60 bat). Classify occurrences against mock and negative control samples using the count_false_occ option of add_filters.pl, producing out/summary_min_readcount_XX/control_counts.tsv. Compute sensitivity TP/(TP+FN) and precision TP/(TP+FP) per pipeline and dataset, and plot with scripts/plt_sensitivity_precision.py. Rationale: control samples determine filtering thresholds and discard false positives that denoising algorithms (Swarm, Unoise, DADA2) cannot catch (contamination, tag-jump, chimeras).

## Evaluation signals

- VTAM attains comparable sensitivity (0.95–1) to DALU and OBIbaR while achieving higher precision per dataset (e.g., precision 1.00 on the five marine fish samples vs 0.66 for OBIbaR and 0.19 for DALU)
- Precision of the DALU and OBIbaR pipelines increases monotonically with the minimum read count cut-off (0, 10, 40, 60)
- Per-dataset optimized cut-offs match VTAM's suggested values: 10 for fish, 40 for shark, and 60 for bat
- control_counts.tsv is produced for each out/summary_min_readcount_XX directory with TP, FP, and FN counts derived via add_filters.pl count_false_occ
- Taxonomic assignment for all three pipelines is done by VTAM's taxassign command to ensure homogeneity of the comparison

## Limitations

- FilterCodonStop and FilterIndel must be skipped for the shark dataset, so pseudogene/spurious-sequence filtering is incomplete for that marker
- Adequate mock communities representative of the targeted group's diversity are required; commercial mocks exist mainly for bacteria and fungi, not metazoa or plants
- The comparison depends on per-dataset optimized VTAM parameters; transferring these thresholds to other datasets is not guaranteed by this skill
